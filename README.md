# The Commons

The Commons is an inventory management and resource-sharing platform for Boston College student organizations.

Student organization e-board members can use The Commons to keep track of their organization’s supplies, equipment, and other shared resources. Instead of purchasing items that another organization already owns, organizations can discover available resources across campus and coordinate borrowing them.

## Purpose

Boston College has hundreds of student organizations, many of which purchase and store similar items. However, there is no centralized way for organizations to know what resources already exist or who owns them.

The Commons is designed to:

- Help organizations manage their own inventory
- Make shared resources easier to discover
- Reduce unnecessary purchases and waste
- Encourage collaboration between student organizations
- Create a clearer record of borrowed and returned items

## Who It Is For

The Commons is intended for verified Boston College student organization e-board members and administrators.

E-board members join their registered organization and manage the resources owned by that organization. Administrators oversee organizations and have access across the platform.

## Main Features

- Sign in using a Boston College Google account
- Join a registered student organization
- Manage organization inventory
- Browse resources across participating organizations
- Check items in and out
- Track item availability and borrowing information
- Register and manage organizations through administrator access

## Project Status

The Commons is currently under active development. Core inventory management, organization membership, authentication, and authorization are implemented. Resource-sharing workflows and deployment infrastructure are still being developed.
